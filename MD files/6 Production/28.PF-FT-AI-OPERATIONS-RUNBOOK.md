# 171. Final Operations Principles

1. **Always identify the environment before taking action.**
2. **Always identify the deployed version and configuration.**
3. **Use correlation IDs to trace incidents end-to-end.**
4. **Diagnose before changing production.**
5. **Contain security and data-integrity incidents immediately.**
6. **Never bypass APIM authorization.**
7. **Never manufacture authorization claims.**
8. **Never disable guardrails to force completion.**
9. **Never expose secrets or sensitive telemetry.**
10. **Never modify enterprise business rules as an AI workaround.**
11. **Treat enterprise systems as the authoritative source of business data and rules.**
12. **Use controlled retries with bounded attempts.**
13. **Respect idempotency before replaying operations.**
14. **Use DLQ for unrecoverable Service Bus messages.**
15. **Replay only after root cause and idempotency are validated.**
16. **Recover workflows only from valid and compatible state.**
17. **Never fabricate missing workflow or ERC data.**
18. **Validate partial ERC processing before reporting completion.**
19. **Use the agreed 20-team batching approach for large ERC processing.**
20. **Apply the same controlled batching strategy to 100+ officials and other large collections.**
21. **Treat SLM, RAG, Vector, MCP and Enterprise APIs as independently diagnosable dependencies.**
22. **Use Langfuse and platform telemetry to establish evidence for AI failures.**
23. **Do not make Langfuse availability a business dependency unless explicitly required.**
24. **Rollback AI releases as a complete compatibility set when required.**
25. **Verify health after every deployment and recovery.**
26. **Do not close incidents until recovery is demonstrated by metrics and functional validation.**
27. **Every recurring incident must produce a permanent corrective action.**
28. **Every major failure should improve tests, alerts, resilience or documentation.**
29. **Operational procedures must be executable by the support team, not dependent on undocumented developer knowledge.**
30. **Production operations must be secure, controlled, observable, auditable and repeatable.**
