# 117. Final Governance Principles

1. **Every production AI capability must have a business and technical owner.**
2. **Every AI capability must have a documented purpose.**
3. **Every AI capability must be risk-classified.**
4. **Risk determines the depth of governance and approval.**
5. **AI must not replace authoritative enterprise rules.**
6. **Authorization remains deterministic and outside the model.**
7. **Models must be approved before production use.**
8. **Prompts are governed production artifacts.**
9. **Agents are governed software capabilities.**
10. **LangGraph workflows are governed execution artifacts.**
11. **Tools and MCP capabilities are privileged integrations.**
12. **RAG sources require ownership, ACL and quality governance.**
13. **Embeddings and vector indexes require compatibility governance.**
14. **ERC is a governed context artifact.**
15. **Memory and cache require scope, retention and access governance.**
16. **Service Bus events require contract and ownership governance.**
17. **Portal links require approved ownership and environment governance.**
18. **Human oversight must be proportional to AI risk.**
19. **Existing enterprise HIL workflows remain authoritative where applicable.**
20. **Every material AI change requires impact assessment.**
21. **Every production change must be versioned and traceable.**
22. **Every production AI release must pass required evaluation and security gates.**
23. **Third-party AI providers require security, privacy and compliance assessment.**
24. **Governance exceptions must be explicit, approved and time-bound.**
25. **Governance evidence must be auditable.**
26. **AI evaluation is part of governance, not merely testing.**
27. **Security is a mandatory governance dimension.**
28. **Responsible AI must be evaluated according to actual business risk and impact.**
29. **No AI agent may modify its own governance boundaries.**
30. **No AI agent may bypass enterprise authorization or policy.**
31. **Configuration and version governance must identify the exact production state.**
32. **AI executions must be traceable to their release and configuration.**
33. **Model, prompt, agent, RAG and guardrail changes require regression evaluation.**
34. **AI systems must have a defined retirement/deprecation process.**
35. **Governance must remain active throughout the complete AI lifecycle.**
36. **The final objective is controlled, accountable and enterprise-safe AI—not unrestricted autonomous behavior.**
